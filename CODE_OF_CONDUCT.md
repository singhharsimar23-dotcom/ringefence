# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Ringfence is a small project that handles security-sensitive code, so discussion is direct and about the work. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- be respectful, and assume good faith until shown otherwise;
- give and accept criticism of code and ideas, never of people;
- keep reports about security problems private until they are fixed;
- not harass, demean, threaten or discriminate against anyone, in any form.

Unacceptable behaviour includes insults, personal attacks, unwelcome sexual attention, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules.

Report a problem through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/singhharsimar23-dotcom/ringefence/security/advisories/new) or by contacting the maintainer through the GitHub profile on this repository. Reports are handled privately.
