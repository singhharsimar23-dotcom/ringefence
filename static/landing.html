01 · The problem

The email that empties a payroll.

A contractor writes: I've changed banks, please use this account from Friday. In a payroll team that's a routine Tuesday. It's also how payroll gets stolen. The FBI counted $3.05 billion lost to business email compromise in 2025.

02 · Why this track exists

Why this track exists.

Airwallex's own agent tools stop short of sending money out. Kit 06 asks for the controls that make that step safe: the platform can technically make the transfer, so policy has to stop it.

We fired the same payroll many times at once and dropped replies on purpose. The transfer list is the referee.

    09 · Funding

    When an employer is short.

    Harbour can't cover this week in AUD. The agent works out how much and by when, asks for funds, and waits. Pay goes out once the money lands, from Airwallex or from a capped crypto wallet on Metal.

    Harbour's shortfall is never covered with Northwind's or Kestrel's money. Balances are never added up across employers.

    1. harbour / funding1

      Short in AUD. The agent asks for the shortfall and the date it is needed by.

    2. harbour / deposit2

      Deposit arrives, the balance covers the run.

    3. harbour / released3

      Pay is released once the approver signs.

    10 · On chain

    Pay the gap from a crypto wallet, with a cap the chain enforces.

    On Metal testnet, Harbour signs a mandate: at most the shortfall in USDZ, one pull, only to the platform's settlement wallet. The agent can use it once. Anything else, Metal refuses and records the refusal on chain.
